Information used for app locking

With your permission, PinGuard's Android Accessibility service detects foreground app package names so it can display a PIN screen for apps you choose to protect. The service does not request screen-content extraction or read messages or typed text. Protected-app selections remain stored on your device. PIN verification uses a salted PBKDF2 hash stored on your device; your PIN is not sent to an external server.

Advertising and consent

PinGuard includes Google AdMob banner advertising in its dashboard and protected-app PIN screens. Ads are optional for protection to function: an unavailable ad, lack of connectivity or a consent decision does not prevent PIN entry. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process information such as device identifiers, network address, diagnostics, and ad interactions as described in Google's policies. PinGuard does not intentionally include the user's PIN, protected-app list or app transition history in ad requests. Where required, consent is requested through Google's User Messaging Platform. Additional ad-privacy controls appear in the app when required.

Read Google's Privacy Policy and Google's AdMob information for more information.

Retention and removal

Your PIN hash and selected-app list remain in Android app storage until you change them, clear PinGuard's app data, or uninstall the app. Android Accessibility permission can be revoked from device settings at any time. Google's retention for advertising-related data is governed by Google's policies.

Children

This app is not designed for or directed to children under 13.
